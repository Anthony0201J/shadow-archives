## What Beginners Actually Need

Before we dive into reviews, let's cut through the marketing noise. As a beginner, you need exactly four things from a web host:

1. **Reliable uptime** (99.9%+ — anything less means your site goes down regularly)
2. **Easy WordPress installation** (one-click setup, not manual FTP uploads)
3. **Free SSL certificate** (non-negotiable for security and SEO)
4. **Decent support** (you will have questions — live chat response time matters)

Everything else — unlimited bandwidth, unlimited email accounts, free CDN — is nice to have but not a dealbreaker. Most hosts include these in even their cheapest plans.

What most review sites *won't* tell you: **the intro price is not the real price.** Every shared host on this list charges a promotional rate for the first term, then increases dramatically on renewal. We'll show you both prices because your second year matters just as much as your first.

## FAQ

**Can I host a website for free?**
GitHub Pages (which hosts this site!) is free for static sites. For WordPress or dynamic sites, there's no good free option — the "free" hosting providers insert ads, offer terrible performance, and can delete your site without notice. Invest the $2.99/month.

**Do I need hosting if I use WordPress.com?**
WordPress.com is a different product from WordPress.org. WordPress.com handles hosting for you but limits your customization. If you want full control, use WordPress.org (the free software) with a hosting provider from this list.

**What's the difference between shared hosting and cloud hosting?**
Shared hosting puts hundreds of sites on one server — cheap but slower. Cloud hosting (Cloudways) gives you dedicated resources on a virtual server — faster and more reliable, but costs more and requires slightly more technical knowledge.

**How do I move my site to a new host?**
Most hosts offer free migration. Hostinger, SiteGround, Cloudways, and A2 Hosting will move your site for free. Bluehost charges $149.99 for expert migration but offers a free WordPress plugin for DIY transfers.

**Should I buy my domain from my hosting provider?**
The free domain offer is convenient, but we recommend buying domains separately from a registrar like Namecheap or Cloudflare Registrar. This makes it easier to switch hosts later without transferring your domain. If you do use the free domain offer, just remember to transfer it out before renewal.
